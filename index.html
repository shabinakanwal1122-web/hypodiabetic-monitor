    About blood pressure from the camera

    Camera-only blood pressure estimates are still research-grade and can be off by a wide margin, so this app does not show one. For blood pressure trends, log readings from a validated cuff under Readings; the app charts them and uses them in alerts and your score.

    Label scanner

    Photograph a nutrition label or a medicine box/bottle. The app reads it and flags hidden sodium, added sugar, and common food interactions.

    Kinetic Step Engine

    Steps are tied to your meals: a short walk soon after eating helps blunt the glucose rise, and regular movement through the day supports healthy blood pressure.
